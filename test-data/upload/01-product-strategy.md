# Komponist MVP Product Strategy

Komponist is the shared company brain for AI agents. The first release should
prove that teams can turn documents into reviewed, cited organizational context.

## Product direction

Decision: The MVP will extract only Decisions, Goals, Constraints, and Projects.

Decision: Every extracted fact must retain a citation to the source document.

Goal: Recruit ten design partners who use Komponist with a coding agent every week.

Constraint: New facts must enter the human review queue before they become trusted context.

Project: Ship the document-to-graph vertical slice with upload, extraction, review, and cited search.

We will postpone graph editing, billing, and additional integrations until the
core document workflow is reliable.
