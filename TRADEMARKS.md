## Forks and modified distributions

The Apache License permits modification and redistribution of the code. A
materially modified distribution should:

1. use a distinct primary product name;
2. avoid the official logo and app icon as its own identity;
3. state clearly that it is modified and not maintained by the Komponist
   project;
4. retain copyright, license, and NOTICE information as required by
   Apache-2.0.

Functional references such as API package names may be retained when changing
them would break compatibility, provided the surrounding presentation does not
imply that the fork is an official Komponist release.
