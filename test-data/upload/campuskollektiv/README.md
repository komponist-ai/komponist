# CampusKollektiv e.V. — realistic MVP test workspace

Upload the 14 numbered `.md` and `.txt` files together. Komponist supports up to
20 documents per upload. The pack deliberately
contains overlapping information and two versions of the Campus Forum plan.

## What this should exercise

- extraction of Decisions, Goals, Constraints, and Projects;
- exact evidence quotes and line-level source links;
- same-document and cross-document graph relationships;
- role and department-sensitive information;
- a prerequisite that must **not** be rewritten as already completed;
- two versions with changed date, duration, budget, and venue;
- one identical copy with a different filename, which must reuse extraction;
- a noisy chat transcript that should not create durable organizational facts.

## Recommended review flow

1. Upload all numbered files.
2. Confirm the well-supported entities in Review Queue.
3. Reject any vague or duplicated item.
4. Inspect Campus Forum relationships in Graph.
5. Open Versions and compare the two Campus Forum plans.
6. Ask Chat the questions below and inspect every cited passage.

## Questions with expected answers

- **How long does the Campus Forum run?** — 6 weeks.
- **When and where is the Campus Forum?** — 14 November 2026 in the Forum Hall.
- **What is the approved Campus Forum budget?** — €4,800.
- **Who owns sponsor outreach?** — Partnerships.
- **How much does the main sponsorship package cost?** — €1,500.
- **Who can read highly confidential board minutes?** — Board members only.
- **Can department members read other departments' private documents?** — No.
- **What must happen before sponsor data can be imported?** — The data-processing
  agreement must be signed; the import is not claimed to be complete.
- **How many active members are targeted?** — 40 by 31 December 2026.
- **Which plan is newer?** — Campus Forum Plan v2 dated 10 October 2026.
- **What changed between Campus Forum Plan v1 and v2?** — date, duration,
  venue, and budget.
- **Who approved free entry?** — The board, in its 2 October meeting.

All content is fictional test data.
