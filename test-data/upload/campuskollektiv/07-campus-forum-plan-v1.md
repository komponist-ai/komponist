# Campus Forum — draft plan

Project: The Campus Forum project runs for eight weeks and ends with the event on 21 November 2026.

Decision: The draft venue for the Campus Forum is the University Auditorium.

Goal: The Campus Forum welcomes 180 attendees and 12 nonprofit organizations.

Constraint: The draft Campus Forum budget must not exceed €4,000.

Project: The Events department coordinates venue, programme, volunteers, and accessibility for the Campus Forum.
