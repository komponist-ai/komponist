# Sponsorship

Decision: CampusKollektiv offers one main sponsorship package for €1,500.

Decision: The Partnerships department owns sponsor outreach and records every contact in the approved CRM.

Constraint: A sponsor may not influence speaker selection, member recruitment, or editorial content.

Constraint: Finance must approve every sponsorship invoice before it is sent.

Goal: Partnerships secures three Campus Forum sponsors by 30 October 2026.

The sponsorship goal advances the Campus Forum project.
