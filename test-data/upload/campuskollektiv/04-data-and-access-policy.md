# Information access

Constraint: Board minutes marked highly confidential are visible only to board members.

Constraint: Department-private documents are visible only to members of that department and the board.

Constraint: The IT department may administer systems but may not read department-private content unless a board member grants temporary access for a documented incident.

Constraint: Sponsor contact data may be stored only in the approved European CRM workspace.

Decision: Public event information and published press material are visible to every association member.

Project: The Access Review project audits department membership and confidential-document access once per month.
