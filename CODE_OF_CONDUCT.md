# Komponist Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Komponist to be a welcoming, constructive, and technically rigorous
community. Everyone participating in the project is expected to help create an
environment free from harassment and exclusion, regardless of experience,
identity, background, location, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Explain trade-offs and provide evidence where possible.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's personal or company data.
- Accept maintainer decisions about project scope and move disagreements into
  constructive technical discussion.
- Help make contribution paths accessible to people with different levels of
  experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information, credentials, or non-public communications.
- Impersonation or deliberately misleading claims about project authority.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Using project spaces to promote spam, scams, or unrelated commercial offers.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
community events, and other spaces where someone represents the Komponist
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the maintainers
through the [`komponist-ai`](https://github.com/komponist-ai) organization and
request a private channel. Include links, dates, context, and any relevant
screenshots with unrelated personal information removed.

Maintainers will review reports privately and may remove content, issue a
warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.
They will avoid conflicts of interest and share report details only with people
needed to resolve the incident.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and
focused on protecting the community rather than winning an argument.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
and adapted for the current early-stage project.
