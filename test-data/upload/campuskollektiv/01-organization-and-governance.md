# CampusKollektiv e.V.

CampusKollektiv is a fictional student association that connects students with
local social-impact organizations. Its departments are Board, Events,
Partnerships, Communications, People, Finance, and IT.

Decision: The board consists of chair Lea König, treasurer Amir Haddad, and operations lead Mia Chen.

Constraint: Only the board may approve contracts above €1,000 or commitments lasting longer than one semester.

Constraint: Every payment requires approval by the treasurer and one additional board member.

Decision: Department leads may organize their own work while the board retains responsibility for legal and financial commitments.
