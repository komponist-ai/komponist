# Volunteer operations

Goal: Events recruits 24 volunteers for the Campus Forum by 5 November 2026.

Project: The Volunteer Operations project assigns volunteers to registration, stage support, accessibility, partner care, and cleanup.

Constraint: Every volunteer handling attendee data must complete the privacy briefing before receiving registration-system access.

Decision: The People department runs the privacy briefing and Events records attendance.

The Volunteer Operations project advances the volunteer recruitment goal.
