# Winter semester 2026/27

Goal: CampusKollektiv recruits 40 active student members by 31 December 2026.

Goal: CampusKollektiv signs eight nonprofit partner organizations by 15 January 2027.

Goal: At least 70 percent of active members remain engaged until the end of the winter semester.

Project: The Member Onboarding project introduces a shared welcome session, department matching, and a 30-day check-in.

The Member Onboarding project advances the goal “CampusKollektiv recruits 40 active student members by 31 December 2026.”

Decision: The People department owns member recruitment, onboarding, and retention reporting.
