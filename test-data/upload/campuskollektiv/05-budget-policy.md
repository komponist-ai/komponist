# Finance policy

Constraint: A department may spend up to €200 per purchase from its approved semester budget without a new board vote.

Constraint: Purchases above €200 require written approval from the treasurer before an order is placed.

Constraint: Reimbursement requests must include a receipt and be submitted within 30 days of purchase.

Decision: CampusKollektiv uses the university association account for all incoming sponsorship payments.

Goal: Finance closes every monthly account review by the fifth working day of the following month.
