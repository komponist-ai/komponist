Customer Interview — Northstar Labs
Date: 2026-07-15

The engineering lead said their agents repeatedly miss architecture decisions
that were written in Notion several months earlier. Search alone is not enough;
the team needs to know which information was reviewed and who can trust it.

Goal: Reduce repeated architecture questions in engineering channels by 50 percent.

Decision: The pilot will begin with the platform engineering team and its Notion workspace.

Constraint: Search answers must cite the exact source document and must not use rejected facts.

Project: Run a four-week Northstar Labs design-partner pilot with weekly feedback sessions.

The customer would consider paying once the system reliably retrieves decisions
inside their coding agent without requiring a separate browser search.
